# Brief — Common Table margin dashboard

- **Need:** Leadership at a fictional wholesale bakery needs a weekly Power BI
  view that identifies where margin is drifting and what decision can still
  change the month.
- **Audience:** Managing director, commercial lead, and production lead.
- **Desired decision:** Choose which account or product family receives the
  first margin-recovery action this week.
- **Receiving outcome:** Implement the approved dashboard or slide-ready report
  direction in the receiving analytics surface without making ADS the report
  runtime or data owner.
- **Current constraint:** Revenue, waste, discounting, and delivery costs are
  reviewed in separate reports with no shared decision sequence.
- **Required data:** Net revenue, contribution margin, waste rate, delivery
  variance, product family, account, region, week, target, and data freshness.
- **States:** Loading, no selection, no data for filters, refresh failure,
  row-level-security denial, and stale data.
- **Constraints:** 16:9 report page, keyboard-readable order, not color-only,
  suitable for projection and laptop use.
- **Source/reference rights, provenance, and licensing:** The bakery, metrics,
  direction, and preview are fictional ADS-owned proof; no live data,
  third-party brand, customer truth, or proprietary report asset is included.
- **Ownership boundary:** ADS owns visual hierarchy, report composition,
  typography, color, and expressive direction. The receiving analytics Project
  owns data modeling, refresh, security, and report implementation after
  acceptance.
- **Proof:** Leadership can name the largest actionable margin gap and its owner
  in under two minutes.
- **Receiving project or repository:** The project selected to implement this dashboard.
- **Review mode:** independent
- **Review owner:** ADS Review
- **Receiver acceptance:** The selected analytics owner explicitly accepts any
  cross-owner snapshot.
