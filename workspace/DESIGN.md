# Business Freedom content system story

## Overview

This direction makes one constraint travel through one story. Useful knowledge,
ideas, and cases already exist, but Demand work still waits for Gustav because
visual decisions, versions, production, and approval are fragmented around him.
The visual answer is a **constraint line becoming a reviewed handoff line**.

The film opens in Gustav Online's human, orange public-learning world. A single
terracotta line enters the warm onlinesourdough method field, becomes the
selected Demand constraint, and passes through the smallest complete change.
One contained dark/cyan beat shows AIOS routing context, ownership, and evidence.
ADS then fixes reusable visual direction; ACS owns lineage, independent node
review, production, and supervised handoff; Diffusion Studio renders locally
through its Electron/DAPI authority while a human reviews through the minimal
browser companion. The close returns to warm cream and shows Business Freedom
as retained human choice and reduced founder dependency.

This is neither a generic brand reel nor an ecosystem montage. Every brand or
product appears only when it advances the same Demand constraint.

## Portable direction and ownership

This `DESIGN.md` is the canonical, portable, human-readable visual direction for
the Business Freedom content system story. The browser preview, three SVG
keyframes, selected assets, editable OpenPencil source, and reviewed export are
companions. None replaces this file or creates a receiving runtime dependency.

- **Scope and non-goals:** ADS owns visual hierarchy, brand-role separation,
  composition, typography, color, imagery treatment, interaction and motion
  direction, and selected reusable visual assets. ADS does not own the final
  editorial thesis, hook, script, source-media selection, edit, render,
  packaging, publication, or Diffusion Studio implementation.
- **Review, revision, and acceptance:** Revision `r1` requires direct preview,
  accessibility, visual, OpenPencil, deterministic, and independent design
  review proof. The cross-owner `ADS-HANDOFF/1` remains `PENDING` until Agentic
  Content System records explicit acceptance. A later change requires a new
  revision and re-acceptance.
- **Sibling boundary:** ACS receives ordinary portable artifacts and owns
  editorial/content production. ADS does not invoke ACS, invent its final
  production decisions, or create a deterministic ADS-to-ACS chain. Diffusion
  Studio is a separately owned local production engine, not an ADS feature.
- **Known limitations:** The 39-second words are draft visual-size cues, not a
  final script or public claim. The preview and native export prove visual
  direction, not final video timing, audio, captions, production rendering,
  browser-companion implementation, content performance, or receiver
  acceptance. Any importer approximation is recorded in the handoff.

## Three keyframe / thumbnail concepts

### 01 — The founder-owned queue

Use an orange 16:9 field split by Gustav's portrait. Crop the image large and
human, with the phone and elevator mirror visible enough to feel personal rather
than corporate. The right side is mostly empty, carrying the two-line cue:
`The useful idea is here.` / `The content still waits for Gustav.` Four narrow
warm-paper slips—visual choice, version, production, approval—collect on the
constraint line. A small `GUSTAV ONLINE / DEMAND` label grounds the role.

This is the strongest thumbnail when recognition of the founder bottleneck is
the hook. Do not add logos, agent avatars, abstract AI imagery, or a false metric.

### 02 — One constraint, routed

Use the warm onlinesourdough cream/walnut field. A single terracotta line crosses
five uneven method stations and pauses on `Demand`. The three offer modes sit as
physical workbench objects along the bottom: Resources / DIY, The Fermentary /
done with you, Complete Bake / done for you. The AIOS dark/cyan proof module is
one bounded inset at the right, never the whole canvas.

This is the explanatory middle keyframe. It must show a method and a choice,
not an installed stack. The onlinesourdough mark may appear once at low scale.

### 03 — The reviewed handoff

Return to warm paper. Three asymmetric ownership fields are connected by the
same terracotta line: ADS `direction`, ACS `lineage · node review · supervised
handoff`, and Diffusion Studio `Electron/DAPI render`. A tiny browser companion
sits beside the engine as the human inspection point. AIOS appears underneath
as four dark/cyan evidence tabs, not above the owners. The Complete Bake
in-practice cue alone may carry Arc’IT green/sand. Finish with `Business Freedom`
and `Demand keeps moving. Gustav keeps the choice.`

This is the payoff frame and preferred closing thumbnail. The modular product
labels must remain separate; do not draw a central brain, master dashboard, or
one enclosing stack.

## Brand-role transitions

- **Gustav Online:** `#f36f2b` orange, real portrait, handheld crop, notebook
  gesture, direct human copy. It opens the story and does not become the method.
- **onlinesourdough:** `#f8f2e8` cream, `#5a3d30` walnut, `#b86f36`
  terracotta, Geist Pixel landmarks, physical paper/workbench objects. It owns
  the method and offer menu.
- **AIOS:** `#0e1720` with `#72d9ed`, monospace evidence labels, one 5-second
  bounded proof beat. It routes context and evidence; it is not a public brand
  wash or combined application repository.
- **Arc’IT AI:** `#146e59` green on `#f3ead7` sand, visible only in the Complete
  Bake / in-practice moment. Do not carry green into Resources, The Fermentary,
  AIOS, ADS, ACS, or the closing field.
- **Business Freedom:** Return to warm cream with one green review mark and the
  continuous terracotta constraint line. Freedom reads as retained choice and
  reduced dependency, not hands-off automation.

## Colors

Warm cream is the main story field. Walnut carries the founder-owned queue and
offer/menu structure. Terracotta is the only line allowed to cross brand beats;
it signifies the same business constraint. Gustav orange is a full opening
signal but ends before the onlinesourdough method. AIOS cyan appears only on its
dark inset. Arc’IT green remains confined to Complete Bake in practice. Green
also marks a reviewed state at the final human approval point, paired with text.

Avoid purple AI gradients, blue-to-orange brand blending, neon glows, and color
transitions that imply all owners are one product.

## Typography

Self-host Geist 1.7.0. Geist Pixel carries onlinesourdough landmarks and the
final Business Freedom phrase. Geist Sans carries direct human statements and
explanatory text. Geist Mono carries time codes, owner labels, state, evidence,
and delivery roles. Use display type sparingly, at a maximum of two short lines
per frame. Final ACS copy should preserve direct, spoken specificity rather than
polish the story into generic SaaS language.

## Layout

Use a 1180px desktop reading frame. The active 16:9 storyboard dominates the
first viewport; scene controls sit in one horizontal filmstrip underneath. The
story direction then unfolds as a narrow beat table followed by three large
16:9 keyframes in an offset editorial rhythm. Do not use a uniform three-card
grid: concept 01 is left-heavy, concept 02 is full-width, and concept 03 is
right-heavy with a narrow ownership annotation rail.

At 390px, the active frame keeps 16:9 proportions; copy scales down and the
filmstrip scrolls horizontally with visible labels. The beat table becomes a
list. Keyframe imagery remains complete, and all captions follow in document
flow. No action or ownership label may depend on hover.

## Elevation & Depth

Use paper overlap, crop, thin warm rules, and one 18px frame boundary. Shadows
stay short and warm. The AIOS inset is flat and technical. The Arc’IT practice
window may use a faint sand-to-transparent image scrim. Avoid floating glass
cards, cinematic light leaks, 3D logos, and ornamental depth.

## Shapes

Controls use 8px corners; large storyboard frames use 24px. Founder-owned slips
look like narrow paper tabs, method stations use small squares and rules, and
ownership boundaries are straight fields with one clipped corner. Pills are
reserved for playback state and the final reviewed mark.

## Components

### Storyboard player

The browser preview holds eight scenes and a 39-second duration. `Play story`
advances through the scene sequence once, updates a polite status line, and ends
at `Complete`. `Pause` preserves the selected scene. Scene buttons are native
buttons with `aria-pressed`; the progress bar has an accessible label and value.
Reduced-motion preference disables automatic scene advancement and decorative
line movement, while manual scene selection keeps every scene available.

### Constraint line

One 3px terracotta line crosses the story. It stalls at founder-owned decisions,
becomes the selected Demand constraint, passes reviewed ownership boundaries,
and closes at human approval. It never branches into generic data flow.

### Method stations

Use five uneven stations: `Understand the business`, `Find the constraint`,
`Choose the smallest complete change`, `Build + prove`, `Launch · operate ·
recover`. Offer / Operations / Demand appear only at the second station, with
Demand selected for this story.

### Offer workbench

Use the approved onlinesourdough object assets as source-safe visual cues:
Resources for DIY, the carried shared-worktable image for The Fermentary, and
the connected bake workstation for Complete Bake. The legacy source filename
`inner-circle-lofi-v3-transparent.png` is not public copy; the selected local
companion is renamed `fermentary-dwy.png`.

### Ownership handoff

ADS, ACS, and Diffusion Studio each receive their own field and verb. Boundaries
are labeled `visual direction`, `content lineage + independent review`, and
`local motion/video render`. The final small browser window says `human review`.
Arrows indicate a selected handoff for this outcome, not a required runtime
chain. AIOS supports the relation with ordinary context/evidence references.

### Proof states

The inspectable state lab includes `loading`, `success`, `error`, `empty`,
`permission`, and `offline`. They describe local storyboard evidence, never
customer or production truth. Error and offline states include a recovery
action; permission makes receiver acceptance explicitly unavailable to ADS.

## Motion direction

- Use eight hard editorial cuts with one continuous constraint line; no
  cross-brand morphing.
- Gustav Online's orange opener may have 120ms handheld drift. Remove it under
  reduced motion.
- Method stations reveal one at a time in 180ms stepped rhythm.
- The AIOS proof beat uses a single cyan scan from context to evidence; it does
  not become a terminal montage.
- ADS/ACS/Diffusion ownership fields lock into place at each reviewed boundary.
- End with a 500ms still hold on the warm Business Freedom frame and visible
  human approval point.
- Captions remain readable for at least 1.8 seconds. Final timing and audio are
  ACS production decisions and require review in the rendered cut.

## Accessibility and reduced motion

The final motion piece needs open captions, a transcript, no meaning carried by
color alone, and no essential text below common platform safe areas. Avoid
flashes and rapid cuts. The preview uses semantic header/main/section/footer
landmarks, one H1, ordered H2/H3 headings, a skip link, labeled native controls,
visible 3px focus, descriptive image alternatives, a polite live region, and a
reduced-motion query. Mobile preserves content order and control access.

## Do's and Don'ts

- Do follow one Demand constraint from founder-owned queue to reviewed handoff.
- Do keep Gustav Online human, onlinesourdough methodological, AIOS technical,
  Arc’IT in-practice, and the close warm.
- Do name Resources, The Fermentary, and Complete Bake with DIY, done-with-you,
  and done-for-you roles.
- Do show ADS, ACS, Diffusion Studio, AIOS, Projects, templates, and skills as
  bounded owners or modular products.
- Do end on fewer repeated founder decisions and retained human choice.
- Don't promise autonomous content production, maximum automation, maximum
  volume, measured results, or a self-running brand.
- Don't depict one monolith, installed stack, agent swarm, central brain, or
  deterministic cross-System chain.
- Don't turn this into a logo parade, generic AI reel, SaaS dashboard, or
  uniform three-card ecosystem grid.
- Don't let Arc’IT green leak outside Complete Bake / in-practice.
- Don't let the optional `.op` source or export replace this direction.
