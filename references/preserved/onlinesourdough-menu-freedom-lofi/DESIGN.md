# onlinesourdough Same Bake Lo-fi Menu

## Overview

This direction keeps the approved onlinesourdough low-fi composition, book dimensions, typography, colors, images, shelf behavior, hover interactions, responsive layout, and theme behavior. It uses a compact desktop scroll rhythm and one coherent public story. Today, almost anyone can start building software with AI, but that same access also makes complexity, technical debt, and unclear ownership easier to create. onlinesourdough is the meeting point between business development and software, used to decide what should exist and how it should work. The outcome is positive bandwidth: more time to work on the business, not inside it. A compact shared Offers switcher connects this method-and-guidance offer to the Arc’It implementation offer without changing the book-menu composition.

### Product Brief

- **Product shape:** Public menu for Content, Resources, done-with-you collaboration, and done-for-you delivery across business development and software.
- **Primary workflow:** Understand the promise, recognize the right level of support, and continue to one destination.
- **Constraint:** Preserve the current copy volume, two-column book menu, warm palette, theme behavior, and approximately 390-pixel desktop card height.
- **Avoid:** Generic AI hype, treating faster building as sufficient, new public offers, animated backgrounds, dense navigation, additional marketing sections, or scroll behavior that traps mobile and reduced-motion users.

### Composition Fingerprint

- **Shared shell:** Header, offer field, About sections, and footer use the same 1240-pixel desktop shell as Arc’It, producing 100-pixel outer margins at 1440 pixels. Headlines and paragraphs keep narrower centered reading measures inside it.

- **Page rhythm:** A centered hero, one compact two-row book menu, and a split manifesto in one natural document flow. Anchor links position the requested section beneath the sticky header without taking control of ordinary scrolling.
- **First viewport:** Brand, short promise, and the beginning of the menu depending on height.
- **Content density:** Exactly the approved level: one title, one paragraph, four concise options, and one short closing principle.
- **Typography contrast:** Geist Pixel carries the wordmark, hero statement, and manifesto statement. Geist Sans carries paragraphs and book titles. Geist Mono carries metadata and actions.
- **Image role:** Four original pixel-art scenes replace abstract icons. Content includes a starter jar, Resources includes a bread lame and dough scraper, Inner Circle includes a sourdough slice with coffee, and Complete Bake retains its computer/server/oven scene.
- **Shape language:** Four narrow portrait book covers float in an open two-column field. Each has a narrow spine and one translucent holder with a straight top and rounded lower corners. There are no visible cell containers.
- **Motion character:** The book lifts 30 pixels out of its stationary shelf, the shelf becomes clearer, and the action arrow shifts four pixels. No continuous animation.
- **Signature moment:** Four small book covers rise from the same quiet shelf while the supermarket-mix metaphor expands from a quick first build into the complete business bake.
- **Anti-pattern:** Do not redesign the approved preview when revising the strategy or copy.

### Reference Translation

- **Supplied Cofounder screenshots:** Learn from pixel-art image roles, book covers rising behind a translucent edge, quiet mono metadata, pixel wordmark character, and shallow paper depth. Do not copy its folder icon, chapter illustrations, landscapes, book proportions, navigation, copy, or exact animation.
- **Approved onlinesourdough menu preview:** Preserve its HTML composition and visual decisions exactly. Preview 2 is a controlled copy experiment, not another design exploration.
- **Direct Notion sources, including Freedom-first Business Model, onlinesourdough Offers, and Resources source of truth:** Retain Business Freedom as the deeper outcome, one constraint at a time as the method, Offer/Operations/Demand as the business model, and the four-offer progression. Keep scorecards, internal qualification, AIOS layers, and unvalidated pricing off this compact page.
- **Existing About page and user's current strategic clarification:** Explain the situation before the solution. AI lowers the practical barrier to building software, but it also lowers the barrier to creating complexity, technical debt, and systems without clear ownership. Position onlinesourdough implicitly through `Here, business development and software meet`, avoiding a detached third-person brand description. Use the sourdough metaphor to make the risk concrete, not to replace the business meaning.
- **Existing onlinesourdough icons:** Learn from the sourdough starter, open resources, cutting bread, shaping, and finished-bake subjects. Translate those subjects into small original objects inside the generated scenes; do not rasterize or paste the SVG artwork into the scenes.
- **Generated assets:** The four pixel-art scenes are original and use onlinesourdough colors. They contain no names, logos, or copied UI.

## Colors

Keep the current warm cream, paper, brown, and crust colors. Muted blue appears only inside the generated images and as a keyboard-focus color. It is not a new page-level accent system.

The existing warm dark theme remains supported. Images keep their pale framed background in both themes so their pixel palette does not invert.

## Typography

Self-host the open-source Geist family. Use Geist Pixel Square for the `onlinesourdough` wordmark and the two major editorial statements: `Same bake. Different deliveries.` and `The supermarket mix…`. Use Geist Sans for body copy, book titles, and controls. Use Geist Mono for menu labels, numbers, availability, descriptors, and actions.

Do not use TT Neoris without an acquired webfont license. Its official foundry sells desktop and webfont licenses separately. Geist is distributed under the SIL Open Font License and keeps the preview portable. Do not use Geist Pixel for paragraphs or card descriptions.

## Layout

Use the current centered 920-pixel main panel, but constrain the visually open two-row book arrangement to 840 pixels. Keep the grid only as invisible layout logic: do not draw an outer frame, cell borders, or card backgrounds. A quiet 920-pixel divider above the menu mirrors the divider above the manifesto and separates the hero from the choices. A 28-pixel column gap and 30-pixel row gap pull the four choices into one coherent group while giving books 3 and 4 slightly more air.

Use natural scrolling on every viewport. `Menu` and `About` use `scroll-margin-top` so their labels land below the sticky header. Header `About` scrolls to the inline manifesto. Only the manifesto CTA opens the dedicated About page. A normal anchor click keeps its section behavior, but a browser reload clears the stale hash and restored scroll position so the page starts at the hero. Never restore scroll snapping, mandatory reading positions, wheel interception, or section locking.

Each option remains about 390 pixels tall on desktop. A 322-pixel-tall book cover sits inside a 302×342-pixel stage. Its approximately 278×322-pixel visible proportions read as a real portrait book instead of a square card. The cover sequence is number/status, title, the complete existing description, divider, and image. Keeping all copy above the image prevents the shelf from obscuring it. A 78-pixel translucent shelf crosses only the lower cover and extends slightly beyond its sides.

Use transparent PNG cutouts for all four pixel scenes, scaled with `object-fit: contain` and internal padding. The alpha channel removes the rectangular raster field entirely, allowing the solid `#fffaf1` image well to show continuously behind and between every object. A fine inner border, low-contrast inset shadow, and restrained color treatment make the well feel printed into the cover. Do not add a CSS mask or background gradient: neither can reliably conceal an opaque raster field. The full description remains directly visible; do not hide it behind a tooltip, because touch and keyboard users need the same information without hover.

On mobile, retain two compact columns when there is enough width so the four choices remain scannable without a long scroll. At very narrow widths, reduce image size and copy scale before switching to one column. Mobile always uses natural scrolling.

## Elevation & Depth

The page remains flat. The book cover uses a short paper shadow, while its illustration uses only an inset shadow to communicate a recessed printed panel. The shelf uses one subtle border, a 30%-mixed warm surface, and three-pixel backdrop blur. On hover the surface mix drops to 16% and blur to 1.5 pixels, revealing the book more clearly while the shelf itself remains stationary.

## Shapes

Book covers use 12-pixel radii. The shelf is deliberately square across its top edge and uses 26-pixel radii only on its lower corners, creating a holder rather than another card. CTAs remain plain text. A narrow 13-pixel book spine is the only internal vertical strip.

## Components

### Header

Use the same three-zone shell as Arc’It: the modular pixel loaf mark plus lowercase `onlinesourdough` at left, compact `Offers`, `Menu`, and `About` links centered, and theme control at right. The selected large-pixel mark is a wide, low SVG assembled from 36 separated square modules on an 8×6 grid. Its domed top, flat base, and three stepped diagonal score cuts must read as a sourdough loaf before the wordmark is considered. Header and footer render the mark at the same 44×34-pixel size so black and white use identical module dimensions. Do not render it through a CSS mask. `Menu` links to the four-book menu. On the landing page, `About` links to `#about` and receives active state from the current scroll position. On `about.html`, About keeps page-level active state. Hide the local Menu link on narrow mobile screens while keeping Offers and About available.

The Offers switcher is the shared family component. It opens directly into `Arc’It AI / done for you` and `onlinesourdough / DIY + done with you`, then marks the current offer without changing their order. It has no heading, divider, close button, numbers, or second link strip. Outside click and Escape dismiss it. Its dimensions, type, spacing, keyboard behavior, and theme response match `../arcitai-hero-lab/`. `Complete Bake` and the Arc’It offer path both link to the authoritative Arc’It preview.

### Hero

Use `Same bake. Different deliveries.` Follow it with a compact situation, risk, solution, and outcome sequence. Begin with `Today, almost anyone can start building software with AI.` The same access also makes complexity, technical debt, and systems without clear ownership easy to create. Conclude with `Here, business development and software meet`, then name the work directly: decide what should exist and how it should work. State the payoff as `positive bandwidth: more time to work on the business, not inside it.` Do not describe onlinesourdough in the third person or add a CTA.

### Menu Card

Each established offer remains a book cover with its two-digit position, availability state, title, existing image, one strategy-aligned sentence, and direct action. Keep every description on the same level: what the delivery contains and how the visitor participates. Content provides ideas and recipes for connecting business and software. Resources provides practical guides, patterns, and tools for the visitor's own business. Inner Circle provides direct access, pair programming, and reviews while working together. Complete Bake provides the system built, documented, and handed over so the owner can work on the business instead of inside it. Use the direct actions `Explore content`, `Open library`, `Work together`, and `Start a project`.

### Manifesto

Keep the approved split heading/copy pattern and the line `The supermarket mix is fine. Until the business runs on the bake.` Define “supermarket mix” directly as a template, ready-made tool, or vibe-coded first version. Explain what the shortcut does not create: architecture, documentation, monitoring, and ownership. Define AI-first as starting with the business and its constraint, then using software and AI to remove work that should not depend on the owner. Describe positive bandwidth as the system continuing to move without pulling the owner back into daily operation. This develops the hero's promise without repeating its `work on the business, not inside it` line. End with the consequence: otherwise the shortcut becomes technical debt and another operation to run. Link `About the method` to `about.html`.

### About

The dedicated About page uses the live onlinesourdough About copy as its source: `From demo to production`, `Why sourdough`, `Where the brands meet`, and `The point`. Keep one centered pixel-led introduction followed by four flat, numbered two-column text rows. Do not turn the story into cards, a timeline, or a second sales page. End with `Back to menu`.

### Footer

Both pages close with the same low-fi dark footer: a 44-pixel boule mark paired with a large lowercase pixel wordmark, one quiet divider, `© 2026 onlinesourdough` at left, and the exact ecosystem links `gustavonline ↗` and `Arc'IT AI ↗` at right. The footer echoes Arc’It’s closing weight without importing its Danish landscape asset.

### Interaction Rules

- Keyboard focus uses a visible muted-blue outline.
- Hover lifts the book 30 pixels while the shelf remains still, makes the shelf more transparent, draws a muted-blue cover boundary, and shifts the action arrow by four pixels. On compact two-column mobile layouts the lift is 22 pixels to avoid collisions. It does not add a background behind the option.
- Theme switching changes page tokens, not the generated images.
- Reduced motion removes the hover translation.
- Desktop uses the natural document top followed by compact proximity-based snap points for the menu and books 3 and 4. The manifesto has no separate snap target. Mobile and reduced-motion users retain natural scrolling.

## Do's and Don'ts

- Do preserve the approved preview's visual design, dimensions, book interaction, and theme behavior.
- Do keep the centered Offers switcher consistent with `../arcitai-hero-lab/` so the two previews feel like adjacent offers.
- Do lead with the relationship between business, software, and responsible technical ownership rather than AI as the subject of the page.
- Do keep business development, software, and AI-first practice in one connected story.
- Do retain Business Freedom and bandwidth as outcomes without making them the only public message.
- Do use AI-first once as a practical design principle, not as a trend claim or autonomous future-state promise.
- Do acknowledge that technical debt can turn software itself into an operator trap.
- Do use mix, bake, pantry, bottleneck, and bakery metaphors only where their business meaning is immediately clear.
- Do let the repeated book/shelf behavior, typography, and four images share the low-fi identity.
- Do keep all four choices visually consistent.
- Do show each complete description directly on the cover.
- Do leave open background between the four books; the two-column grid is layout logic, not a visible component.
- Don't copy the supplied folder asset or Cofounder imagery.
- Don't copy the supplied Cofounder shelf SVG, noise filter, or path. The preview uses an original CSS holder derived from the general straight-top/rounded-bottom principle.
- Don't turn the shelf into navigation, a carousel, or a decorative section outside the four existing choices.
- Don't add continuous background animation.
- Don't add more sections or copy blocks to explain the internal framework.
- Don't make broad claims about what AI is, what it will become, or what every business can safely build.
- Don't reduce AI-first to code generation, chat, isolated tools, agents, or maximum automation.
- Don't imply that building more or building faster automatically creates bandwidth.
- Don't introduce tooltips for copy that belongs to the primary menu decision.
- Don't use em dashes in customer-facing copy.

- `assets/content-lofi-v3-transparent.png`: transparent publishing desk with a sourdough starter jar.

- `assets/complete-bake-lofi-v3-transparent.png`: transparent connected delivery workbench.
- `assets/onlinesourdough-mark-large-pixel-v3.svg`: selected runtime mark assembled from 54 large, closely spaced square modules on a wide 10×7 composition grid. Its filled low dome, rounded base, and three continuous negative score cuts preserve the recognisable bread silhouette from the earlier fine-pixel study. Each module occupies 82% of its grid cell, giving the mark Arc’It-scale pixel weight without turning it into an outlined face. Header, footer, and favicon all use this asset; the two visible lockups render it at the same 44×34-pixel size.
- `assets/onlinesourdough-mark-large-pixel-v4.svg`: archived 9×7 outline experiment. The open centre and added crumb cluster began to resemble a facial expression instead of bread.
- `assets/onlinesourdough-mark-large-pixel-v2.svg`: archived 8×6 large-pixel experiment. Its flat rectangular lower half and disconnected negative cells weakened the bread silhouette.
- `assets/onlinesourdough-mark-v3.svg`: archived finer 12×8 loaf. It established the correct bread silhouette but its individual pixels were smaller than the Arc’It module language.
- `assets/onlinesourdough-mark-v2.svg`: archived symmetrical boule experiment. Its tapered base read too much like a cake, so neither page loads it.
- `assets/onlinesourdough-mark-v1.png`: archived generated boule exploration. It is retained as process history but is not loaded by either page.
- `assets/fonts/`: self-hosted Geist Sans, Geist Mono, Geist Pixel Square, and their license from Geist 1.7.0.
- Raster assets were edited with the built-in image generation tool on a flat chroma background, converted to alpha with the imagegen helper, edge-contracted by one pixel to remove green fringe, and resized to 640×640 PNG.
- The original boule study was generated as a strict 1-bit logo study on a flat chroma background. The selected v3 runtime mark preserves that filled silhouette as a code-native pattern made from large, tightly spaced square SVG modules, so the bread remains sharp and legible without CSS-mask or raster-loading dependencies.
