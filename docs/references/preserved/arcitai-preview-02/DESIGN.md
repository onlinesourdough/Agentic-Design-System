# Arc’It AI Preview 02 — Focused Onboarding

## Overview

Arc’It AI is a builder studio for AI-native products, internal tools, and software systems. Preview 02 is deliberately unlike a broad agency homepage. It is one narrow, self-contained story: identify the promise, understand the work through a short film, inspect three public examples, and begin a qualified inquiry without choosing among services.

### Product Brief

- **Audience:** Founders and small teams with a useful software idea, an operational bottleneck, or a product workflow worth improving.
- **Primary workflow:** Read the promise, activate the overview, inspect public cases, complete the embedded first inquiry step, then add qualification context.
- **Core message:** Turn useful ideas into software people actually use.
- **Brand feeling:** Quiet Scandinavian editorial design, optimistic lofi software-world imagery, current AI capability, soft sand, Arc’It green, and living Danish flower colors.
- **Avoid:** Wide agency navigation, service grids, generic proof strips, fake testimonials, invented outcomes, giant dark closing bands, feature-card stacks, scroll-jacking, and multiple competing actions.

### Composition Fingerprint

- **Page rhythm:** A centered brand seal floats above a narrow 760-pixel column. The opening is a single editorial block followed immediately by a 16:9 overview frame. Public work occupies one featured case and two compact notes. The embedded inquiry closes the story. There is no conventional full-width header or separate footer campaign section.
- **First viewport:** A visible, masked Arc’It landscape occupies the upper hero as spatial atmosphere rather than a thumbnail. A small capacity status, large centered serif promise, short explanation, two actions, and a dark founder-VSL frame appear in the first desktop viewport.
- **Content density:** The page contains four conceptual moments—promise, film, work, inquiry—but only three visible page sections. Supporting explanations stay inside the film dialog, case subpage, and inquiry continuation.
- **Typography contrast:** An OS editorial serif carries the central promise and section statements. Geist Sans carries useful explanation. Geist Mono carries status and operational detail. Geist Pixel appears only inside the small Arc’It brand seal and footer.
- **Image role:** Arc’It panorama version 7 is the hero atmosphere with enough scale to show coast, fields, modular systems, and harbor. The VSL remains an independent dark video frame reserved for real founder footage. Existing Arc’It software-world illustrations support case cards on the separate case page. They are thematic brand images, never represented as project screenshots.
- **Shape language:** One soft video frame, quiet rounded case surfaces, small functional controls, and a compact seal. Avoid browser chrome, angled panels, hard pixel borders, and nested dashboard cards.
- **Motion character:** The poster makes one restrained scale shift on hover. Dialogs use native browser motion. Fixed project access changes position between desktop and mobile. There is no parallax, timeline animation, 3D object, or scroll-controlled scene.
- **Signature moment:** The persistent green project action always leads to the visible embedded inquiry, while the overview action reveals the studio logic without leaving the page.
- **Anti-pattern:** Do not restore Preview 02’s former wide two-column hero, three-cell proof strip, horizontal case table, or dark full-width CTA footer. Those elements made it read as a conventional agency template.

## Colors

The page keeps Arc’It’s original deep green, sand, ivory, and small natural-spectrum details. Lavender and sun yellow are landscape and micro-signal colors rather than interface fills.

- Use **background** continuously across the page so sections never become a conventional stack of color bands.
- Use **surface** for the intake card, case cards, and form controls.
- Use **surface strong** for dark text and VSL depth.
- Use **primary** for the persistent onboarding action, play control, links, and selected words.
- Use **sun** only for the capacity point and tiny landscape details.
- Use **lavender** in the image and one tiny principle signal; never use it as a large button or heading fill.
- Status colors always pair with text.

## Typography

- Iowan Old Style with Baskerville and Georgia fallbacks creates the calm editorial center of gravity seen in the reference principle without copying its exact typeface.
- Geist Sans carries explanatory copy, case titles, forms, and dialog text.
- Geist Mono carries capacity, time codes, case categories, facts, buttons, and field labels.
- Geist Pixel appears only in the 9-pixel brand-seal label and footer wordmark.
- The main statement scales from 68 pixels on desktop to about 45 pixels at small widths.
- Body copy stays between 14 and 17 pixels with restrained line length.

## Layout

The landing page uses a single centered 760-pixel column and ordinary 18-pixel desktop gutters.

- The brand seal occupies the first 126 pixels of vertical rhythm.
- The hero itself is centered and begins with a visible masked landscape atmosphere spanning the page width. The background composition preserves the upper-left sun and right-side harbor while supporting text contrast.
- The founder-VSL frame uses a full-column dark 16:9-like surface on desktop and a content-driven 4:3-like surface on mobile. It is not a landscape poster and receives real captioned footage later.
- Public work starts 76 pixels after the overview. One 330-pixel featured card combines text and a thematic Arc’It image; two compact notes follow in a two-column row.
- The embedded inquiry uses a two-column text/form composition within the same narrow page. On mobile it becomes linear.
- `cases.html` narrows further to 720 pixels and stacks three substantial case cards with thematic images and factual definitions.
- The project action remains fixed at the top-right on wide screens and becomes a full-width bottom dock below 1040 pixels.
- No horizontal overflow is acceptable at 390 pixels.

## Elevation & Depth

- The VSL carries the strongest shadow and one dark photographic gradient.
- The intake card has a shallow atmospheric shadow because it is the primary conversion object.
- Case cards use boundaries and surface contrast rather than floating depth.
- The green project dock may cast one small shadow so it stays legible over page content.
- Dialogs use a blurred dark backdrop and one restrained surface shadow.

## Shapes

- The brand seal is a soft 18-pixel square with an inset sand border; it is not a postage-stamp imitation.
- Video and substantial case surfaces use an 18-pixel radius.
- Controls and fields use a 10-pixel radius.
- Only the media play control and capacity status are circular or pill-like because their semantics justify the shape.
- Avoid decorative slashes, serrated stamp edges, browser windows, terminal panels, and ornamental rails.

It does not introduce Orange Blob’s horse, stamp geometry, year number, or orange outline.

### Persistent Project Action

Use `Start a project` in one Arc’It-green fixed control. It anchors to the visible embedded inquiry instead of opening a modal immediately. On small screens it becomes a bottom dock. The same label is used in the hero and overview continuation.

### Opening Promise

Use the capacity message `Selected capacity · taking on one new build`, the statement `Turn useful ideas into software people actually use.`, one direct explanation, and only two actions: `Start a project` and `View case studies`.

### Founder VSL

Reserve a dark founder-VSL frame for real talking-head footage, captions, and restrained supporting visuals. A compact play control opens the three-part explanation—Find the leverage, Build the system, Leave it usable—inside a dialog. The preview explicitly states that the footage is not yet supplied. Panorama version 7 stays in the hero background instead of impersonating video.

### Public Work

Feature Pi Desktop in one split case surface using `arcitai-world.jpg` as thematic Arc’It artwork. Show Agentic Video Editor and DESIGN.md Workbench as two compact factual notes. All three lead to `cases.html`; do not fabricate metrics, clients, screenshots, or testimonials.

### Case Study Page

Use one editorial introduction and three stacked case cards. Each card includes a thematic Arc’It image, purpose, product shape, public status, and source repository. The fixed project action returns to the landing-page inquiry. Clearly describe images as illustrative in alt text.

### Embedded Inquiry

The landing page shows the first inquiry step directly: first name, last name, work email, and project type. Valid completion opens a second-step dialog containing company, website, role, company size, budget, and project context while preserving the first-step values.

- The preview sends and stores nothing.
- Lock the page at its exact scroll position while a dialog is open.
- Restore focus when closed.
- Close via the explicit control, Escape, or backdrop.
- Keep visible two-pixel focus treatment.

### Footer

End with one hairline, the Pixel wordmark, Email, GitHub, YouTube, and year. Do not add another campaign statement or dark section.

## Do's and Don'ts

- Do keep the landing page narrow, centered, and conversion-specific.
- Do place the VSL immediately after the promise and actions.
- Do keep the embedded inquiry visible rather than hiding all onboarding in a modal.
- Do use only factual public work and clearly thematic imagery.
- Do preserve the balanced green, lavender, blue, red, white, and yellow landscape palette.
- Don't copy Orange Blob’s proprietary brand assets, copy, photography, testimonials, metrics, or client proof.
- Don't add back a wide navigation bar, proof strip, case table, or full-width dark CTA.
- Don't invent adoption, savings, revenue, clients, testimonials, or outcomes.
- Don't turn every sentence into an independent card.
- Don't add scroll choreography or decorative animation.
