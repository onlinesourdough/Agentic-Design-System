# Adaptive reference route

Use this reference only when a caller supplies visual evidence or the brief
does not yet resolve a visual direction. It extends the primary ADS route; it
is not another public skill, design source, media library, or vendor catalogue.
`direct`, `discover`, and `explore` are decisions inside the existing ADS
understand → design/render → critique/revise → accept/handoff flow. They are not
new lifecycle phases and do not import a caller's orchestration vocabulary.

## Intake boundary and precedence

Keep caller screenshots, URLs, channels, landing pages, brand assets, and prior
accepted productions in the caller's owner. ADS records only a locator; kind;
visual role; revision or hash when available; provenance and rights; extracted
positive and avoid signals; confidence; and target surface. Never copy or
dereference caller media merely to preserve the run. If inspection is needed,
inspect it in place and retain only the pointer and derived signals.

Resolve conflicts in this order:

1. explicit owner constraints, including must-use and avoid decisions;
2. current accepted brand truth and prior accepted production;
3. task-specific supplied references;
4. the small ADS-owned baseline as fallback or comparison vocabulary; then
5. bounded new discovery for one concrete unresolved role.

Write the selected decisions into canonical `DESIGN.md`. Reference metadata and
route proof stay subordinate run evidence and must not become a second visual
specification.

## Choose exactly one mode

- `direct`: owner/brand/prior/task evidence resolves the job and visual
  direction without material conflict. Do not evaluate unrelated source
  candidates. The baseline may supply words, never an override.
- `discover`: exactly one concrete visual role remains unresolved, such as
  motion language, data-density treatment, or an accessible interaction
  pattern. Evaluate current license, revision, maintenance, accessibility,
  framework/runtime fit, and reuse boundary. Select at most one primary source
  plus one optional adapter; otherwise record rejection or `blocked`.
- `explore`: no caller direction evidence exists or materially different
  directions remain. Compare two compact directions by default, or three only
  when the brief explicitly needs the bounded additional comparison. Do not
  combine contradictory concepts into a sprawling moodboard. Selection is not
  canonical until it reaches `DESIGN.md` and Review.

Current, dated X practitioner material may be the primary discovery signal for
contemporary design-with-agents practice. Preserve author, date, locator, and
the concrete inspectable claim; label it as a signal rather than a standard.
Follow consequential claims to first-party evidence, and require rights,
accessibility, framework/reuse fit, plus proof on the rendered task before
adoption. Older academic evidence may corroborate historical limitations but
must not stand in for current frontier-model evidence.

## Six-lens baseline

These are internal conversation aids, not a page, catalogue, registry, or
owner-maintained choice system. When `explore` is useful, proactively show only
the two most relevant cards in chat (three only for an explicit bounded need),
using each card's lens, visual frame, signals, useful contexts, and avoidance.
The caller never has to browse, configure, or maintain the baseline.

### Baseline lens: quiet-editorial

- **Visual frame:** A whitespace-led reading plane, human-scale text rhythm,
  and one modest focal anchor.
- **Signals / useful for:** Calm hierarchy, generous negative space, human
  texture; long-form ideas, reports, and content-led service surfaces.
- **Avoid:** Fragile tiny type, ornamental emptiness, or treating “premium” as
  silence.

### Baseline lens: precise-product

- **Visual frame:** Neutral interface structure with crisp grouping and visible
  state changes.
- **Signals / useful for:** Purposeful density and state clarity; applications,
  settings, and operational workflows.
- **Avoid:** Dashboard chrome without a job, decorative controls, or density as
  a competence signal.

### Baseline lens: evidence-led

- **Visual frame:** One decision-first number or chart, direct labels, and a
  tabular supporting rhythm.
- **Signals / useful for:** Traceable comparison and restrained data display;
  dashboards, reports, and status reviews.
- **Avoid:** Equal-card walls, unlabeled color encodings, or decorative charts.

### Baseline lens: narrative-frame

- **Visual frame:** A clear sequence with one focal idea per slide or frame.
- **Signals / useful for:** Contrast and pacing; presentations, explainers, and
  designed story progression.
- **Avoid:** Deck wallpaper, competing messages, or transitions without
  narrative work.

### Baseline lens: warm-tactile

- **Visual frame:** Warm neutrals, modest material texture, and legible traces
  of process or craft.
- **Signals / useful for:** Human scale and readable imperfection; physical
  goods, hospitality, process, and craft.
- **Avoid:** Nostalgia before legibility, fake wear, or texture pretending to be
  authenticity.

### Baseline lens: cinematic-human

- **Visual frame:** A person–environment relationship, contextual framing, and
  sparse overlays.
- **Signals / useful for:** Restrained grading and contextual b-roll; human
  stories, documentary material, and video direction.
- **Avoid:** Unlicensed likenesses, mood replacing meaning, or grading that
  flattens real skin and place.

The six frames are original ADS guidance, not images, templates, brands, fonts,
palettes, components, or production assets. Use them only when
higher-precedence evidence needs fallback or comparison vocabulary. Record the
selected lens and reason in route proof, then author a task-specific direction;
the card itself never becomes the outcome.

## Review evidence

Keep route evidence in the Codex conversation for the current task: name the
brief, selected mode, higher-precedence evidence, any bounded discovery or
cards shown, and the resulting direction. Do not create an executable route
model, schema, registry, or durable baseline selection store. Review the
resulting task preview at its actual target surfaces. Model interpretation of
a reference is a hypothesis until the rendered output and named Review confirm
the intended signals.

Tokens, assets, exports, and OpenPencil remain optional replaceable companions.
The route does not change ADS/Project/ACS ownership, invoke a sibling, or create
an automatic recursive request.
