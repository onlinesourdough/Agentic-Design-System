# Brief — resources.onlinesourdough

- **Need:** Give founders and small teams a calm, navigable place to name the
  business constraint in front of them, choose what should change, and find the
  system, guidance, foundation, or working example that supports the next
  decision.
- **Audience:** Solo business owners who want to become more techy and build
  more of their business with AI and software, plus small teams making the
  same practical decisions about ownership and responsibility.
- **Desired action:** Start with `Start Here`, understand the Business Freedom
  Ecosystem method, then open the smallest useful child resource or toolkit.
- **Receiving outcome:** Implement the accepted Resources visual direction,
  shell, responsive states, and selected reusable assets in the receiving
  product without depending on ADS or its creative tools at runtime.
- **Business vocabulary:** The umbrella method is **Business Freedom
  Ecosystem**. AI, agents, and software are the primary solution mechanism;
  **AI Workspace** is the configured technical base inside it; and **AIOS
  Template** is the open-source foundation. The delivery routes are **Resources
  · do it yourself**, **The Fermentary · done with you**, and **Complete Bake ·
  done for you**. Arc’IT AI remains a separate In Practice external destination.
  Do not create a Managed Operations offer or category.
- **Three value layers:** Business architecture finds the right constraint
  through one fixed, individually applied journey. AI, agents, and software are
  the primary solution mechanism; AI Workspace is the customer’s concrete
  configured technical base inside the ecosystem. Business bandwidth/freedom is
  the outcome, shaped through keep, remove, automate, or delegate. Resources,
  The Fermentary, and Complete Bake are delivery modes for the same layers, not
  separate sidebar taxonomies. The preview must not imply a meeting cadence,
  capacity promise, or guaranteed outcome.
- **Locked IA:** The sidebar has four static group headings with these children:
  `Explore` → `Start Here`, `What’s New`, the quiet expandable `AI Literacy`
  subsection, and `Newsletter`; `Business Freedom Ecosystem` → `Understand your
business`, `Choose what to change`, `Build the solution`, `Launch and run it`;
  `Open Source` → `AIOS
Template`, `Solution Template`, `Skills`; `In Practice` → `Arc’IT AI`, `AIOS
Desktop`, `Power BI Template`. AI Literacy’s disclosure contains `Overview`,
  `Context`, `How AI works`, `Tools`, `Agents`, and `Skills`; it is the only new
  Explore surface and its children are visually quieter than top-level links.
- **AI Literacy content:** AI Literacy is one concise English business-first
  reference for understanding technical concepts well enough to choose and
  build responsibly. Its five independently useful concepts are `Context`, `How
AI works`, `Tools`, `Agents`, and `Skills`. It is not a course, module
  sequence, separate product, or AI Engineer profession track.
- **AI Literacy page contract:** `/explore/ai-literacy` stays minimalist with
  one short intro, a `What you’ll understand` section with at most three bullets,
  and a `Concepts` section of five unnumbered links. Concept routes are
  `/explore/ai-literacy/context`, `/explore/ai-literacy/how-ai-works`,
  `/explore/ai-literacy/tools`, `/explore/ai-literacy/agents`, and
  `/explore/ai-literacy/skills`. Each uses the shared page template with one
  short summary and 3–5 concise key points. No video or explainer block appears
  until a real playable source exists, and no published video is claimed.
  AI Workspace remains a possible configured base, not the first mandatory
  step. Old Models, Workflows, numbered, and Open Source AI Literacy routes
  resolve to not-found.
- **External links:** Newsletter is exactly
  `https://gustavonline.com/newsletter`; Arc’IT AI is exactly
  `https://arcitai.com/`. Both open in a new tab with
  `rel="noopener noreferrer"`.
- **Underlying curriculum:** The existing 00–08 sequence remains semantically
  intact beneath Business Freedom Ecosystem. `Understand your business`
  contains 00–02, `Choose what to change` contains 03–04, `Build the solution`
  contains 05–06, and `Launch and run it` contains 07–08. `00` is the
  orientation resource `Start Here`; 01–08 retain their existing meaning.
  Contextual toolkits appear as child resources inside these four ecosystem
  pages. They are draft/internal examples, not a new top-level category.
- **Homepage folders:** The four folders are `Explore`, `Business Freedom
Ecosystem`, `Open Source`, and `In Practice`, in that order. Each folder is a
  direct link to its group’s first child; the In Practice folder therefore opens
  the verified Arc’IT AI external URL safely.
- **Visual direction:** Preserve the approved warm cream and walnut shell,
  Geist Pixel / Geist Sans / Geist Mono typography, pixel-folder identity cue,
  active `resources-hero-agentic-v2.png` poster, and silent local
  `resources-vsl-motion-preview-v2.mp4`. Preserve sidebar/topbar interaction,
  light/dark mode, responsive drawer/collapse, search, account, help, keyboard
  focus, and reduced-motion behavior.
- **Shelf lock:** `.folder-shelf` remains `display: none`. The visible
  `.folder-shelf-foreground` stays in front at `z-index: 5`, `height: 78px`,
  `bottom: -2px`, with no backdrop blur, about 30% surface fill, 48% border,
  and a subtle inset/external shadow. Do not regress this implementation.
- **Homepage content:** Keep the honest six-second video player with native
  pause, seek, fullscreen, and replay controls. Position Business Freedom
  Ecosystem for solo business owners who want to become more techy and build
  more of their business with AI and software. Keep the hierarchy business
  first, name AI Workspace and AIOS Template plainly, and avoid AI hype,
  invented proof, public inventory, commercial claims, or technical status.
- **Page presentation:** Start Here, What’s New, AI Literacy, Business Freedom
  Ecosystem stages, and their child/toolkit pages use one calm reading column,
  short headings, plain links or bullets, generous whitespace, and no visible
  curriculum prefixes, numbered UI, card-heavy rows, repeated status/meta
  clutter, or unloaded preview scaffolding. The shared page/subpage contract is
  H1 plus one short summary, meaningful H2 sections only, H3 only for real
  nested content, plain paragraphs/bullets/links, and no prose card surfaces.
  Existing curriculum and draft/access data remain intact underneath.
- **Route behavior:** Internal child pages use one shared minimal data-driven
  page/subpage template with full `Resources / Group / Child` topbar
  breadcrumbs. The AI Literacy disclosure is keyboard accessible, expands by
  default on its overview/concept routes, and gives `aria-current="page"` only
  to the exact selected nested link while the parent exposes truthful
  `aria-expanded` state. The preview includes representative ecosystem step
  and toolkit routes, the AI Literacy reference and concept routes, search
  loading/results/empty states, account
  validation/success, help validation/success, draft/permission/offline
  guidance, active/hover, visible focus, and not-found states.
- **Constraints:** Use only local assets in the preview. Do not invent public
  URLs, published inventory, commercial proof, secrets, runtime logic,
  integrations, deployments, or application implementation for the receiving
  Resources project.
- **Source/reference rights, provenance, and licensing:** ADS owns the direction
  and generated visual assets; the user-owned carried folder asset and local
  Geist fonts retain the provenance and licenses documented in
  `assets/README.md`, `assets/fonts/LICENSE.txt`, `docs/preservation.md`, and
  `docs/THIRD_PARTY.md`. HeroUI informed an original local state adaptation;
  no component source or ACS production truth was copied.
- **Ownership boundary:** ADS owns visual direction, brand/style/voice
  expression, graphic composition, motion language, and reusable design
  assets. The receiving Resources Project owns implementation. If ACS receives
  a selected visual snapshot, ACS owns editorial content, edit/render
  execution, packaging, and publication; ADS does not perform those steps.
- **Proof:** At roughly 1440×1000 and 390×844, a first-time visitor can see the
  four folders, identify the method and technical vocabulary, open Start Here,
  open the verified external links, use the video, search, collapse/reopen the
  navigation, open the English AI Literacy reference and a concept page, use
  the mobile drawer, switch theme, and traverse the same content without
  horizontal overflow.
- **Receiving project or repository:** The project selected to implement the
  accepted design; this example remains a portable design workbench artifact
  until accepted.
- **Review and acceptance owner:** ADS Review approves this direction and its
  optional native-source boundary; the named receiving owner explicitly
  accepts any cross-owner `HANDOFF.md` snapshot.
