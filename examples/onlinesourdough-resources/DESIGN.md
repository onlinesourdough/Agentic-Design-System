# resources.onlinesourdough Resources

## Overview

### Design read

Resources is a calm business decision and delivery library. Its first job is to
make the next decision legible: understand the business, choose what should
change, build the smallest useful solution, and launch or recover it with clear
responsibility. Business Freedom Ecosystem is the umbrella and the method. It is
tech-focused without making AI Workspace the product or first step.

The visual language is a quiet documentation shell with four physical folders,
an honest local motion preview, and one ruled update section. It is not a
marketplace, SaaS dashboard, generic course catalogue, or offer catalogue.

| Value layer                | Short reading                                                                         |
| -------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Business architecture      | Find the right constraint; fixed shape, individually applied.                         |
| AI, agents, software       | Primary solution mechanism; AI Workspace is the configured base inside the ecosystem. |
| Business bandwidth/freedom | The outcome; work design keeps, removes, automates, or delegates.                     |

Resources · do it yourself, The Fermentary · done with you, and Complete Bake ·
done for you are delivery modes for the same layers. Arc’IT AI remains a
separate In Practice external destination. No cadence, capacity, or guaranteed
outcome is claimed.

### Product brief

- **Audience:** Solo business owners who want to become more techy and build
  more of their business with AI and software.
- **Primary action:** Open `Start Here`, then select the smallest useful
  Business Freedom Ecosystem stage or contextual toolkit.
- **Locked IA:** `Explore` contains `Start Here`, `What’s New`, `AI Literacy`,
  and `Newsletter`; `Business Freedom Ecosystem` contains `Understand your
business`, `Choose what to change`, `Build the solution`, and `Launch and run
it`; `Open Source` contains `AIOS Template`, `Solution Template`, and `Skills`;
  `In Practice` contains `Arc’IT AI`, `AIOS Desktop`, and `Power BI Template`.
- **AI Literacy:** This English Explore area is a quiet expandable reference for
  understanding technical concepts well enough to choose and build responsibly.
  Its five independently useful concepts are `Context`, `How AI works`, `Tools`,
  `Agents`, and `Skills`. The disclosure contains an `Overview` link followed by
  those five concept links; the parent is a neutral documentation group label
  with a chevron, while only its nested links receive hover and active surfaces.
  Expanding the disclosure opens `/explore/ai-literacy` and selects `Overview`;
  collapsing keeps the current route. AI Literacy connects to Business Freedom
  Ecosystem, AIOS, and AI Workspace; AI Workspace remains a possible configured
  base, not the product or first mandatory step.
- **Home proof:** The first view shows the method in business-first language,
  the four folders, the active v2 computer scene in a real HTML video player,
  and a clear path to `Start Here`.
- **Overview proof:** `Start Here` concisely frames the current constraint, the
  smallest useful change, and the delivery mode. It mentions
  AI Workspace or AIOS Template only when they help the decision; it is not
  brochure copy.
- **External proof:** Newsletter uses
  `https://gustavonline.com/newsletter`; Arc’IT AI uses
  `https://arcitai.com/`. Both links open in a new tab with
  `rel="noopener noreferrer"`.

### Underlying curriculum

The existing 00–08 sequence stays beneath the four Business Freedom Ecosystem
pages rather than becoming sidebar taxonomy:

| Ecosystem page           | Child resources                                                                 |
| ------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Understand your business | 00 `Start Here`, 01 `Find the current constraint`, 02 `Map the business system` |
| Choose what to change    | 03 `Decide what should change`, 04 `Place ownership`                            |
| Build the solution       | 05 `Specify the smallest complete result`, 06 `Build and prove it`              |
| Launch and run it        | 07 `Review and simplify`, 08 `Ship, operate, and recover`                       |

Each page also exposes contextual toolkit child resources. Their draft/internal
and access truth remains in the data and handoff contract; the visible UI uses
plain links without repeated status labels or helper scaffolding.

### AI Literacy content contract

`AI Literacy` is a quiet expandable Explore subsection and a small business-first
reference, not a course or separate product. Its overview is minimal: one short
intro, a `What you’ll understand` section with at most three concise bullets,
and a `Concepts` section with five unnumbered independently useful links. The
disclosure contains `Overview` plus the same five concept links:

| Concept      | Route                               |
| ------------ | ----------------------------------- |
| Context      | `/explore/ai-literacy/context`      |
| How AI works | `/explore/ai-literacy/how-ai-works` |
| Tools        | `/explore/ai-literacy/tools`        |
| Agents       | `/explore/ai-literacy/agents`       |
| Skills       | `/explore/ai-literacy/skills`       |

Concept pages show their concise summary and 3–5 key points as ordinary reading
content. No video or explainer block appears unless a real playable source is
connected; the preview does not claim a published video.
The AI Literacy disclosure is keyboard accessible: Enter and Space toggle it,
and expanding from a collapsed state navigates to `/explore/ai-literacy` with
`Overview` selected. It expands by default on the overview and concept routes.
The parent is a neutral documentation label with truthful `aria-expanded` and
no current-page semantics; only the exact selected nested link receives
`aria-current="page"`. Breadcrumbs include `Resources / Explore / AI Literacy /
Concept`. Old Models, Workflows, numbered, and Open Source AI Literacy routes
are not canonical and resolve to not-found.

### Composition fingerprint

- **Page rhythm:** A narrow documentation rail, quiet utility header, centered
  video frame, compact two-by-two folder stage, then one plain `What’s New`
  section. Explore and Business Freedom Ecosystem pages use one calm reading
  column with short headings, plain links or bullets, and generous whitespace.
- **Content density:** Navigation is useful but restrained; the reading surface
  is open. Only decision-useful labels remain. Curriculum prefixes, numbered UI,
  repeated status/meta lines, card-heavy rows, and decorative state panels stay
  out of the visible page.
- **Typography contrast:** Geist Pixel identifies the wordmark and one display
  statement. Geist Sans carries headings, prose, navigation, and controls.
  Geist Mono marks routes, dates, access, status, and the 00–08 sequence.
- **Image role:** `resources-hero-agentic-v2.png` is the active poster for the
  six-second `resources-vsl-motion-preview-v2.mp4`. The poster keeps the warm
  laptop/workbench scene, two small pixel companions, and one unbranded cabled
  local-AI node. The v1 poster and MP4 remain retained but inactive. The
  supplied transparent pixel folder stays a small sidebar identity cue.
- **Shape language:** Thin warm rules, paper indexes, square-ish controls,
  walnut folder pockets, seams, tabs, subtle grain, and a low transparent
  foreground shelf. Surfaces represent an object or a reading state.
- **Motion character:** Rear folder papers lift 26px, 38px, and 52px on
  hover/focus over 300ms with `cubic-bezier(0.23, 1, 0.32, 1)`. The visible
  shelf stays still. Drawer, search, history, video, and theme transitions are
  functional and respect reduced motion; touch remains a direct link.

## Colors

Warm cream is the page field; paper white marks reading surfaces; walnut carries
folder fronts; terracotta is reserved for small active accents; blue is reserved
for visible keyboard focus. Light folder fronts use `#5a3d30`, `#624333`,
`#694938`, and `#56392d` with `#fffaf1` text. Dark mode uses the same warm
hierarchy with `#80583e`, `#885c42`, `#7a503a`, and `#76503b` with `#fff2df`
text. Dark mode is a warmer reading field, not a neon alternative. Muted text
and focus rings remain readable in both themes.

## Typography

Geist Pixel is intentionally scarce: wordmark, display statement, and selected
folder titles. Geist Sans is the default for headings, body copy, controls, and
navigation. Geist Mono is for route labels, dates, access/status, shortcuts,
and the ordered 00–08 sequence. Never use pixel type for long paragraphs.

## Layout

Use a 1120px maximum page shell with a 250px desktop sidebar and a 62px utility
header. Every ordinary route uses one shared `--reading-width: 720px` track:
the topbar breadcrumb, page header, route layout, and page actions all center
against the same effective width inside `.site-main`, so their left edges stay
aligned when the sidebar opens or collapses. The track can contract at the
narrow desktop edge to leave room for the utility rails; at widths up to 1350px
the topbar uses a compact flex fallback and smaller icon controls to avoid
overlap. The home video is centered above a compact browser frame. The folder
stage is 772px wide with two columns, a 60px desktop column gap, and a 92px row
gap; below 760px it becomes one column with a 68px row gap. At 900px the
sidebar becomes a drawer. Mobile keeps the same reading order without
horizontal overflow.

## Elevation & Depth

Use borders, tonal shifts, and physical paper overlap before shadows. Each
folder front is a walnut pocket with a tab, seam, date/index, short preview,
CTA, and tiny Resources-native pixel accent. The rear papers reveal their
section-specific metadata on hover/focus. The foreground shelf is intentionally
quiet and translucent, not a blurred glass effect. The video uses a theme-aware
scrim so live HTML copy remains readable over the poster; native controls own
playback, pause, seek, fullscreen, and replay.

## Shapes

Controls use an 8px radius, reading panels 12px, and the lower shelf a broad
24px lower curve. Folder tabs, paper corners, metadata lines, and divider rules
are the visual vocabulary. Child pages do not use status cards, decorative pills,
or repeated rounded containers.

## Components

### Navigation

The four group names are visible static headings. Their ordinary children are
visibly indented and carry active-route treatment. Explore has `Start Here`,
`What’s New`, a keyboard-accessible `AI Literacy` disclosure immediately before
`Newsletter`, and no other new top-level child. The disclosure is a neutral
text-and-chevron group label with no hover or active surface; it contains
`Overview`, `Context`, `How AI works`, `Tools`, `Agents`, and `Skills`, whose
nested links alone receive link hover and active surfaces. The parent button has
no active/current-page treatment; it only communicates disclosure state with
its chevron and `aria-expanded`. Expanding from the collapsed state navigates to
`/explore/ai-literacy` and selects `Overview`; collapsing keeps the current
route. It is expanded by default on its routes, and nested links remain
visually quieter.
Open Source retains only its three existing children. The one Home link is the
`Resources.onlinesourdough` brand. The topbar contains the sidebar toggle,
browser-history back/forward controls, icon-only Search, verified external
Newsletter, Account, and Theme actions with accessible names and tooltips.
Newsletter and Arc’IT AI use safe new-tab behavior. Account and Need help are
pinned to the rail bottom; the mobile drawer adds a close action and backdrop.

### Home folder shelf

The four direct-link folders are Explore, Business Freedom Ecosystem, Open
Source, and In Practice in that order. They route to Start Here, Understand your
business, AIOS Template, and Arc’IT AI respectively. Each folder has three
layered paper sheets, a walnut pocket, visible date/index, short preview, CTA,
and a small pixel accent. Hover and keyboard focus lift only the papers and
folder front; touch remains a direct tap target.

The shelf implementation is deliberately exact: `.folder-shelf` remains
`display: none`; `.folder-shelf-foreground` is the visible rail at `z-index: 5`,
`height: 78px`, `bottom: -2px`, with no `backdrop-filter`, approximately 30%
surface fill, a 48% border treatment, and a subtle inset/external shadow. It is
`pointer-events: none` and stays in front while the papers move. Do not restore
the removed rear shelf or describe the foreground as a two-layer blur.

### Home hero and reading rows

The active local v2 poster and silent v2 MP4 sit inside a compact browser/window
frame. Real HTML copy identifies the method, the AI/software mechanism, the
configured technical base, and the business-freedom outcome; the centered play
control starts the six-second preview and hides the cover copy while it plays.
Native controls remain visible and usable.
The media is replaceable design-preview content, not a claim about a final VSL,
customer result, or published capability. One plain `What’s New` section sits
below the folders; its status/access truth remains in data rather than repeated
as visible metadata.

### Shared page and state behavior

Every internal child uses the same data-driven page/subpage template: its own
H1, one concise summary, meaningful H2 sections, plain paragraphs, compact
bullets, and plain child links. H3 is reserved for real nested content. The
single calm reading column uses no ordinary prose cards/panels, no kicker/meta/
status labels, no generic About/Return scaffolding, and no repeated rules; one
divider is allowed only between genuinely separate long sections. A video block
appears only when a playable source exists. Business Freedom Ecosystem pages
show clean plain links or bullets for their existing child resources and
contextual tools; the underlying 00–08 data/routes and draft/access truth remain
intact without visible prefixes or status noise. The preview expresses the
contract through one shared `resourcePageTemplate` and one shared
`resourceSubpageTemplate`, with route data choosing content rather than bespoke
layout. Search includes one overview and five concept results without
duplicates, while unknown nested routes resolve to the truthful not-found state.

Search exposes a labeled field, loading state, useful results, and an empty
state with a recovery hint. Account and Need help expose validation errors and
local success feedback. Draft, locked/permission, internal, and offline states
are written as truthful prose; no published inventory or technical status is
implied. Not-found, hover, active, focus, theme, drawer, collapse, video pause,
and reduced-motion states are visible in the preview.

## Receiving project page contract

The receiving project should implement one route shell, such as
`app/(resources)/template.tsx` or its framework equivalent. That template owns
page padding, the centered 720px reading width, and rhythm. Shared
`ResourcePage` and `ResourceSubpage` components own the semantic H1, summary,
ordered body, and child presentation. Route files choose data and parent
relations, not bespoke widths, orientation, or margins. The receiving app
should expose the same `reading-track` contract to its topbar breadcrumb,
`PageHeader`, `RouteLayout`, and ordinary `PageActions`; the shared token is
720px at wide desktop and contracts only when the viewport or utility rails
require it.

The minimal relation-shaped interface is record kind (`page`, `subpage`, or
contextual `toolkit`), parent relation, slug, title, summary, ordered
body/children, access/status, and sort order. Breadcrumbs derive from parent
relations. The static preview models this contract with one shared
`resourcePageTemplate` and route data; it is not a runtime dependency.

## Do’s and Don’ts

- Do keep the four folder destinations literal and visible near the first
  reading surface.
- Do keep the 00–08 sequence under its four Business Freedom Ecosystem pages,
  with contextual toolkits inside those pages.
- Do present Explore, AI Literacy, and ecosystem pages as quiet Notion-like
  reading surfaces with plain links, bullets, and useful whitespace.
- Do use business-first vocabulary: Business Freedom Ecosystem, AI Workspace,
  AIOS Template, Resources, The Fermentary, and Complete Bake.
- Do preserve the supplied pixel folder, active v2 media, warm shell, visible
  focus, and exact foreground shelf behavior.
- Don’t use AI in the ecosystem name or create Managed Operations as an offer or
  category.
- Don’t invent public URLs, commercial proof, published inventory, technical
  status, or a generic SaaS/course-marketplace taxonomy.
- Don’t turn every region into a rounded card, expose curriculum prefixes as UI,
  add a second navigation model, or make the shelf a blurred backdrop.
