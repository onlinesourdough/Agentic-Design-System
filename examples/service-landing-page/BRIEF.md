# Brief — Plainwork Studio

- **Need:** A small operations studio needs a landing page that explains a
  concrete offer without sounding like another AI consultancy.
- **Audience:** Owners of 5–30 person service businesses whose work is spread
  across inboxes, spreadsheets, and individual memory.
- **Desired action:** Book a 30-minute operations map.
- **Current constraint:** The offer sounds abstract before prospects can see
  what the engagement produces.
- **Required content:** One offer, three deliverables, a sample engagement,
  fit/not-fit guidance, and a booking CTA.
- **States:** Hover, active, focus, form validation, submission progress,
  success, and booking-service failure.
- **Constraints:** Plain English, no invented testimonials or performance
  claims, responsive from 360px, reduced-motion friendly.
- **Proof:** A first-time visitor can explain the offer and find the booking
  action within ten seconds.
- **Receiving project or repository:** The implementation project selected by its owner.
